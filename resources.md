I wanted to share the resources that got me through some tough courses: 

### Linear Algebra:
- [Interactive Linear Algebra](https://textbooks.math.gatech.edu/ila/): This is one of the best linear algebra textbooks I have ever read. It is not as proof-heavy, but instead, it helps readers develop an intuitive understanding of the concepts. This textbook was instrumental in helping me understand linear algebra and actually retain all that I learned.
- [MathTheBeautiful](https://www.youtube.com/c/MathTheBeautiful): Professor Grinfeld does an amazing job of teaching each concept instead of just going through the computations. Highly recommend.

### Algebraic Structures:
- [Socratica, Abstract Algebra](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IP7nW_hKB7I&list=PLi01XoE8jYoi3SgnnGorR_XOW3IcK-TP6): This entire playlist is just phenomenal. Does a great job of breaking down complex topics.

### Real Analysis:
- [Uniform Continuity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_continuity): As a visual learner, the first gif on this page helped me visualize/understand UC.
